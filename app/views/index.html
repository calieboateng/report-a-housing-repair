Report a housing repair

Use this service to report a repair in your home if you rent from a council or housing association in England.

You can report:

  • damp or mould
  • leaks or water coming in
  • problems with heating or hot water
  • problems with electrics or lighting
  • broken doors, windows or locks
  • other repairs inside or outside your home

We’ll send your report to your landlord and give you a reference number. It takes around 5 minutes.
