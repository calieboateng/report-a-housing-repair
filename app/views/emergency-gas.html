Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. It’s free and open 24 hours a day.

While you wait:

  • open doors and windows if you can
  • turn off the gas at the meter if you know how
  • do not use light switches, matches or anything that could make a spark

If you or anyone else feels unwell, call 999.

When it’s safe

Once the gas emergency service has made your home safe, you can report any repairs that are still needed.
